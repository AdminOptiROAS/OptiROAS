Delivered ROAS

The revenue your ads actually collected

Meta, Snapchat and TikTok count an order the moment it's placed. In the GCC many of those are cash-on-delivery orders that get refused at the door or returned. OptiROAS takes them out.

Example: 100 orders a platform reports

Returned−8
Delivered and paid70

Delivered ROAS2.1×
At a 45% margin, break-even is 2.22×. This campaign looked profitable but isn't.

How it works

  1. Export your ordersFrom Salla, Zid, Shopify or your COD tool, with order status and the source or UTM columns. Arabic column names are fine.
  2. Add them next to your ad exportsOptiROAS matches each order to its platform by source, and to its campaign by utm_campaign.
  3. See ROAS on delivered ordersFor every platform and campaign: reported vs delivered ROAS, refusal and return rates, and orders still in transit.

    Platforms

    Getting the right file from each platform

    OptiROAS reads exports from Meta, Google Ads, TikTok, Snapchat, LinkedIn, Microsoft, Pinterest and X, plus store orders from Salla, Zid and Shopify.

    Step-by-step: choose platforms, export, upload and check, then generate.

    Menus move around; if a button has a slightly different name, look for Export or Download near the date picker. A breakdown by day unlocks the trend checks.

    Meta Ads (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp)
    1. In Ads Manager, open the Ads tab and pick the date range (30–90 days works best).
    2. Breakdown → By time → Day. Optionally add By delivery → Placement or Impression device.
    3. Columns should include Amount spent, Impressions, Reach, Link clicks, Results or Purchases, and Purchases conversion value for online stores.
    4. Reports → Export table data → .csv or .xlsx.
    Google Ads
    1. Open Campaigns and set the date range. Optionally Segment → Time → Day.
    2. Make sure Cost, Impr., Clicks, Conversions and Conv. value are shown. Add Search lost IS (budget) for scale checks.
    3. Click the download icon → .csv or Excel.
    4. For keyword waste, also download Insights and reports → Search terms for the same dates and add both files.
    TikTok Ads
    1. In Ads Manager, open Campaign and switch to the Ad level.
    2. Set the date range and choose a breakdown by Day.
    3. Custom columns: Cost, Impressions, Clicks (destination), Conversions, Reach, and a purchase value column if you sell online.
    4. Click Export → CSV or Excel.
    Snapchat Ads
    1. In Ads Manager → Manage ads, open the Ads tab and set the date range.
    2. Break down by Day and include Spend, Paid impressions, Swipe ups, Purchases and Purchase value.
    3. Use Export → CSV.
    LinkedIn, Microsoft, Pinterest, X and others

    Any campaign performance export works. LinkedIn: Campaign Manager → Export → Campaign performance, with a daily time breakdown. Microsoft Ads: download the campaigns grid. For any other platform, the file needs at least:

    Campaign Spend or Cost and ideally Date Impressions Clicks Conversions Conversion value.

    If a file is recognised as the wrong platform, change it in the file list after uploading.

    FAQ

    Questions before you upload

    Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    No. Files are read by your browser on your device. We only record that you ran a report, to count your free plan, never what was in it. When you’re signed in, the open report stays in this browser until you sign out or press Start over.

    How many free audits do I get?

    Each account gets one free audit, once. After your first upload you can make up to 3 fixes, such as adding, removing or re-assigning a file, before you confirm. Once confirmed, the files are locked. You can save the action plan and the full summary as PDF once each, and save the report to your account to come back to it. Starter and Pro are unlimited and include platform connections and the spend calculator.

    Why don't the platforms' numbers add up to my sales?

    Each platform counts the conversions it believes it caused, so the same sale is often claimed twice. Enter your actual revenue to see the gap and your blended MER.

    What's the difference between Starter and Pro?

    Starter connects up to 3 ad platforms, so you can re-run your audit any time without exporting files, and emails you a follow-up 7 days after each saved report. Pro connects up to 7 and adds the live dashboard with today’s numbers and alerts on what changed. Both include one login per account.

    What is delivered ROAS?

    Platforms count an order the moment it's placed, even if it's later refused at the door or returned. Delivered ROAS uses your store's order statuses so you see revenue that was actually collected.

    Can I get the site and report in Arabic?

    Yes. Choose العربية in the settings menu at the top of the page. Numbers, campaign names and currencies stay exactly as they are.

    Do I need a revenue column?

    No. Lead, messaging and app campaigns are judged by cost per result instead of ROAS, using the result type in your export.

    What if my currency isn't US dollars?

    Currency is detected from your files and can be changed in the report settings. Export every platform in the same currency for accurate totals.

    Data deletion

    How to disconnect OptiROAS and have your data deleted. Last updated 26 October 2026

    OptiROAS is a product of Baan Technology Services FZC, which handles all deletion requests.

    What OptiROAS holds

    If you connected an advertising account, we hold read-only copies of your ad account names and IDs, campaign and ad details and performance data, plus your name, email and account ID from your login. If you only used the free audit, your files never left your browser and there's nothing to delete. Full details are in the privacy policy.

    1. Disconnect Meta

    Either of these stops OptiROAS from reading any more data straight away:

    • In OptiROAS: open Account settings → Connected platforms and choose Disconnect next to Meta.
    • In Facebook: go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, select OptiROAS and choose Remove. You can also manage this from Meta Business Suite under Business settings → Integrations.

    Disconnect Google Ads

    • In OptiROAS: open your account and choose Disconnect Google Ads. This also revokes OptiROAS's access on Google's side.
    • In Google: go to myaccount.google.com/connections, select OptiROAS and choose Delete all connections.

    Disconnect Snapchat

    • In OptiROAS: open your account and choose Disconnect Snapchat. This deletes OptiROAS's stored access straight away.
    • In Snapchat: open your Snapchat account settings, find OptiROAS in your connected apps and remove it.

    Disconnect TikTok

    • In OptiROAS: open your account and choose Disconnect TikTok. This revokes OptiROAS's access and deletes the stored access straight away.
    • In TikTok: in TikTok Ads Manager or Business Center, open the list of authorized apps, find OptiROAS and remove it.

    2. Ask us to delete your data

    Email admin@optiroas.com with the subject "Data deletion request" from the email address on your OptiROAS account, or tell us which Facebook account or ad account IDs you connected.

    • We confirm we've received your request within 3 working days.
    • We delete your connected ad account data, access tokens, audit history and profile within 30 days, and email you when it's done.
    • If you remove OptiROAS from Facebook or Google without emailing us, we delete the data received from that platform automatically within 30 days.
    • We keep only what the law requires us to keep, such as invoices and payment records, and nothing else.
